# Part V — Integration

Integration produces rest by functional unity. Multiple elements resolve as a unit, frame, interlock, system, role, constraint structure, equilibrium, or convergence.

Integration begins when relation and correction are no longer enough. Terms may depend on one another, and they may adjust, but the integrative seed appears only when multiplicity must function as one organized whole. The invariant is not yet present at the seed. It must be cultivated through terminal mode, orientation, and operator dominance.

The Integration family is defined negatively against relation and closure. Relation binds terms without necessarily making a whole. Closure ends demand without necessarily organizing multiplicity. Integration produces rest when plurality becomes functionally unified.

---

## I37 — Integration · Toward · Is → Functional Union

The seed of Functional Union is multiplicity beginning to act as one. Several elements are present, but completion requires them to stand as a single functioning unit.

Cultivation first collapses terminal mode to Is. The landing is a state of unity, not a held frame and not an enacted interlock. Orientation collapses to Toward, because attention gathers into one focal unit. Operator dominance collapses to Integration, because the landing is produced by functional unity rather than direct relation or final assertion.

**Functional Union** is the form of completion in which multiple elements stand as one functioning unit. Its formula is: X and Y form a unified unit.

It must be Functional Union because Is leaves a state, Toward gathers multiplicity into one unit, and Integration supplies functional unity. The elements remain intelligible only as a unit at rest.

It is not Direct Pairing, because relation is insufficient; the parts must function as one. It is not Frame Consolidation, because plurality is not held as a frame. It is not Structural Interlock, because the act of interlocking is not the remainder. It is not Established Fact, because the work is integration, not final assertion. It is not Identity Collapse, because the terms do not become indistinguishable under closure.

Use Functional Union when completion requires parts to stand as one function. Reject it when terms merely pair, remain framed, interlock as action, settle as fact, or collapse into identity.

Use Frame Consolidation when completion requires multiple elements to remain together inside one frame. Reject it when they become one unit, interlock as action, counterbalance, collapse into identity, or merely extend across range.
