# Part II — Expansion

Expansion produces rest by lawful opening. Something extends, branches, distributes, or increases degrees of freedom without losing structural intelligibility.

The Expansion family begins when a distinguished term opens beyond its prior standing. But expansion is not looseness and not mere increase. It is opening under constraint. Each invariant in this part names a finite way that extension, divergence, branching, range, distribution, possibility, transition, or growth can come to rest.

Expansion is defined negatively against cut, dependence, correction, unity, and final closure. It does not primarily separate. It does not primarily relate. It does not primarily repair. It does not primarily integrate. It opens.

---

## I10 — Expansion · Toward · Is → Outward Expansion

The seed of Outward Expansion is the first pressure of a term exceeding its prior boundary. Something begins to open beyond its limit.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to Is. The landing is a state, not held plurality or action. Orientation collapses to Toward, because attention gathers into one stabilized extension. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because opening is what produces rest.

**Outward Expansion** is the form of completion in which a single term stands as extended from its prior boundary. Its formula is: X expands outward into Y.

It must be Outward Expansion because Is leaves state, Toward gathers extension into one landing, and Expansion supplies lawful opening.

It is not Divergent Movement, because multiple paths do not remain. It is not Branching Proliferation, because no branches are produced. It is not Range Extension, because no retained span is dominant. It is not Edge Growth, because growth action is not the landing. It is not Identity Constriction, because the movement opens rather than narrows.

Use Outward Expansion when something opens outward while still resolving as one state. Reject it when the act preserves routes, branches, distributes, grows as action, or narrows.

## I11 — Expansion · Toward · With → Divergent Movement

The seed of Divergent Movement is one source opening into more than one direction.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to With. Multiple paths remain. Orientation collapses to Toward, because the source remains focal. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because opening into multiplicity produces the landing.

**Divergent Movement** is the form of completion in which multiple paths diverge from a single origin while remaining anchored to it. Its formula is: X diverges from Y along multiple paths.

It must be Divergent Movement because With holds plurality, Toward preserves source-focus, and Expansion supplies opening.

It is not Outward Expansion, because plurality remains. It is not Branching Proliferation, because production is not enacted. It is not Range Extension, because the landing is source-oriented rather than span-oriented. It is not Multiple Pathways, because the source has not been released.

Use Divergent Movement when completion requires anchored divergence. Reject it when extension is singular, branch-production occurs, the field spans as range, or source focus falls away.

## I12 — Expansion · Toward · Does → Branching Proliferation

The seed of Branching Proliferation is a source becoming productive. Opening begins to generate outcomes.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to Does. Branches must be produced. Orientation collapses to Toward, because the branches remain source-oriented. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because lawful opening into multiplicity produces the result.

**Branching Proliferation** is the form of completion in which a source actively produces multiple outcomes. Its formula is: X branches into Y1, Y2, Y3.

It must be Branching Proliferation because Does requires enactment, Toward requires focal source, and Expansion supplies opening.

It is not Divergent Movement, because plurality is produced rather than merely held. It is not Outward Expansion, because the result is not a single state. It is not Field Distribution, because the spread is branch-like rather than field-like. It is not Generalized Production, because production is expansive rather than closure-level generality.

Use Branching Proliferation when one source actively generates multiple branches. Reject it when the result is merely extended, merely divergent, field-distributed, or generalized under closure.

## I13 — Expansion · Across · Is → Path Variation

The seed of Path Variation is extension encountering route difference. Something can reach its landing by another path.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to Is. The remainder is a route condition. Orientation collapses to Across, because the routing span remains active. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because the route-space opens.

**Path Variation** is the form of completion in which extension remains open across alternative routes. Its formula is: X reaches Y via alternative path Z.

It must be Path Variation because Is leaves a state, Across retains the span, and Expansion opens possible routing.

It is not Range Extension, because plurality of reach is not the remainder. It is not Field Distribution, because no spread is enacted. It is not Multiple Pathways, because the span remains retained rather than released. It is not Sequential Linkage, because dependency is not dominant. It is not Directional Pivot, because no corrective redirection occurs.

Use Path Variation when completion depends on an alternative path rather than a new endpoint. Reject it when routes are held as range, enacted as distribution, released as future options, ordered as dependency, or redirected as correction.

## I14 — Expansion · Across · With → Range Extension

The seed of Range Extension is opening across scope, distance, or reach.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to With. Plural reach remains. Orientation collapses to Across, because attention retains a span. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because opening produces the range.

**Range Extension** is the form of completion in which extension holds across a range while retaining plurality. Its formula is: X extends across range Y.

It must be Range Extension because With holds plurality, Across retains range, and Expansion supplies opening.

It is not Outward Expansion, because the result is not one extended state. It is not Path Variation, because the issue is reach rather than route. It is not Field Distribution, because the spread is not enacted. It is not Divergent Movement, because the landing is not anchored to a source. It is not Formal Correspondence, because no relational pattern governs the landing.

Use Range Extension when something extends across a retained range. Reject it when there is one extension, route variation, enacted distribution, source-anchored divergence, or pattern correspondence.

## I15 — Expansion · Across · Does → Field Distribution

The seed of Field Distribution is opening becoming spread. Something begins to distribute across a field.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to Does. Distribution must be enacted. Orientation collapses to Across, because the span remains active. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because opening produces field-like spread.

**Field Distribution** is the form of completion in which opening actively distributes across a field-like span. Its formula is: X distributes as field Y.

It must be Field Distribution because Does requires action, Across retains the field, and Expansion supplies opening.

It is not Range Extension, because distribution is enacted. It is not Path Variation, because the issue is not route condition. It is not Branching Proliferation, because the spread is field-like rather than branch-like. It is not Role Integration, because no system role is fulfilled. It is not Generalized Production, because the result is spread, not closure-level production across cases.

Use Field Distribution when an action spreads across a field. Reject it when the act merely extends, varies route, branches, integrates, or generalizes.

## I16 — Expansion · Away · Is → Multiple Pathways

The seed of Multiple Pathways is release from one route, one source, or one span. The field opens into available futures.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to Is. The remainder is a state of possibility. Orientation collapses to Away, because prior containment falls away. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because opening degrees of freedom produces rest.

**Multiple Pathways** is the form of completion in which a structure permits multiple future outcomes after release. Its formula is: X permits multiple future Y options.

It must be Multiple Pathways because Is leaves possibility as state, Away releases prior constraint, and Expansion supplies opening.

It is not Divergent Movement, because no source remains focal. It is not Path Variation, because no retained span dominates. It is not Range Extension, because plurality is not held as range. It is not Branching Proliferation, because branches are not produced. It is not Established Fact, because alternatives remain open.

Use Multiple Pathways when the field comes to rest as permissive multiplicity. Reject it when paths are source-anchored, span-retained, range-held, branch-produced, or closed into fact.

## I17 — Expansion · Away · With → Continuous Diffusion

The seed of Continuous Diffusion is opening without fixed boundary. One state begins to pass into another.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to With. More than one state remains structurally present. Orientation collapses to Away, because fixed boundaries fall away. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because transition is produced by opening.

**Continuous Diffusion** is the form of completion in which one state transitions smoothly into another without fixed boundaries. Its formula is: X gradually transitions into Y.

It must be Continuous Diffusion because With holds plurality, Away releases fixed containment, and Expansion supplies boundaryless opening.

It is not Gradual Differentiation, because the dominant transformation is opening rather than difference. It is not Formal Correspondence, because no relational pattern is primary. It is not Directional Reversal, because no corrective reversal occurs. It is not Dynamic Equilibrium, because the plurality is not oscillating balance. It is not Retrospective Reinterpretation, because no completed state is reassigned after the fact.

Use Continuous Diffusion when completion preserves transition without discrete endpoints. Reject it when continuity is primarily difference, pattern, reversal, equilibrium, or retrospective reassignment.

## I18 — Expansion · Away · Does → Edge Growth

The seed of Edge Growth is a boundary being exceeded by expansion. Something opens past its prior limit.

Cultivation collapses terminal mode to Does. Growth must happen. Orientation collapses to Away, because the prior edge is released. Operator dominance collapses to Expansion, because opening beyond boundedness produces the result.

**Edge Growth** is the form of completion in which a structure actively grows beyond its previous limit. Its formula is: X grows at its periphery into Y.

It must be Edge Growth because Does requires enacted result, Away releases the prior boundary, and Expansion supplies opening.

It is not Outward Expansion, because the landing is enacted growth rather than state. It is not Field Distribution, because the span is released rather than retained. It is not Continuous Diffusion, because transition is not merely held. It is not Limit Recalibration, because the boundary is exceeded rather than reset. It is not Convergent Stabilization, because the result is growth rather than stable integration.

Use Edge Growth when expansion enacts growth past an edge. Reject it when the result is merely larger, distributed, transitional, recalibrated, or stabilized.
