# The Atlas of Remainders

## Book II of *How Sentences Come to Rest*

**A field guide to the 54 forms of completion.**

---

Book I establishes the ground: an intelligible act completes when it comes to rest.

Book II asks what remains.

An invariant is the stable remainder produced when an open field contracts through exactly one operator, one orientation, and one terminal mode.

**Operator · Orientation · Terminal Mode → Invariant**

---

## How to Read Book II

Each invariant begins from a seed pressure. The seed is then cultivated by collapsing terminal mode, orientation, and operator dominance until one coordinate survives.

The invariant is not named by resemblance. It is verified by exclusion: neighboring landings must fail before the correct landing can stand.
